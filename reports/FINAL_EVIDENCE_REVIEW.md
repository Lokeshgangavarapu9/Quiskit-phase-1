### 2.1 First-Principles Physical Model & Continuum Targets
- **Hamiltonian:** 4-spin Transverse-Field Ising Model:
  $$\hat{H} = - J \sum_{i=0}^{N-2} \hat{Z}_i \hat{Z}_{i+1} - J_{\text{cross}} \hat{Z}_0 \hat{Z}_{N-1} - h \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} \hat{X}_i$$
  with $J=1.0, h=0.8, T=1.0$, initial state $|\psi(0)\rangle = |1010\rangle$.
- **Exact Continuum Diagonalization:** Independently evaluated via SciPy Padé matrix exponential (`scipy.linalg.expm(-1j * H * t)`):

    - $\langle \hat{Z}_2(1.0) \rangle_{\text{exact}} = +0.574157$
    - $\langle \hat{Z}_3(1.0) \rangle_{\text{exact}} = -0.257957$
    - $\langle \hat{M}_z(1.0) \rangle_{\text{exact}} = -1.53 \times 10^{-16} \equiv 0.000000$ (zero to numerical machine precision).
  - **Periodic Extension ($J_{\text{cross}} = 0.5$):**
    - $\langle \hat{Z}_0(1.0) \rangle_{\text{exact}} = +0.421887$
    - $\langle \hat{Z}_1(1.0) \rangle_{\text{exact}} = -0.584543$
    - $\langle \hat{Z}_2(1.0) \rangle_{\text{exact}} = +0.584543$
    - $\langle \hat{Z}_3(1.0) \rangle_{\text{exact}} = -0.421887$
    - $\langle \hat{M}_z(1.0) \rangle_{\text{exact}} = +2.78 \times 10^{-17} \equiv 0.000000$.
- **Mathematical Proof of Magnetization Parity:**
  The spatial reflection parity operator $\hat{\mathcal{P}}$ maps spin index $i \to 3 - i$. Because $\hat{\mathcal{P}} \hat{H} \hat{\mathcal{P}}^\dagger = \hat{H}$ (both open-chain nearest-neighbor and cyclic cross-term $Z_0 Z_3 \to Z_3 Z_0$ commute with reflection) and $\hat{\mathcal{P}}|1010\rangle = |0101\rangle = \bigotimes_i \hat{X}_i |1010\rangle$, expectation values satisfy:
  $$\langle \hat{Z}_0(t) \rangle = -\langle \hat{Z}_3(t) \rangle, \quad \langle \hat{Z}_1(t) \rangle = -\langle \hat{Z}_2(t) \rangle \quad \forall t$$
  Hence, $\langle \hat{M}_z(t) \rangle \equiv 0.000000$ identically for all $t$ under both open and periodic boundaries. This proves tracking $\langle M_z \rangle$ alone masks Trotter dynamics, establishing $\langle Z_0(t) \rangle$ as the authoritative observable.
